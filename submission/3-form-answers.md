The form asks two things about your own work in Figma Make: what you changed from the agent's first draft, and what you fixed after tapping through it. Only you know those answers, so:

- **Replace `[what you changed]`** with the real changes you made to Figma Make's first draft. Examples of the kind of thing to write: "the colors and font to match my design", "the job cards to show a match score", "the tracker from a list to four stages".
